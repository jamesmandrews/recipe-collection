# Baked Meatballs in Cream of Celery Gravy

> Old-school family casserole — browned ground chuck meatballs baked in a creamy celery-and-ketchup gravy.

## Details

| | |
|-----|-----|
| **Source** | Family recipe (originally labeled "Swedish Meatballs") |
| **Cuisine** | American |
| **Servings** | 8 |
| **Prep Time** | 20 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 45 minutes (15 browning + 30 baking) |
| **Oven Temp** | 325°F / 165°C |
| **Storage** | Fridge 3-4 days • Freeze 3 months • Reheat covered at 325°F / 165°C until hot through |

## Ingredients

### Meatballs
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Ground chuck | 2 lb | 900 g |
| Dry bread crumbs | 1 cup | 110 g |
| Eggs | 2 | 2 |
| Evaporated milk | ¼ cup | 60 ml |
| Cooking oil, for frying | 2-3 tbsp | 30-45 ml |

### Sauce
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Condensed cream of celery soup, undiluted | 2 cans (10.5 oz each) | 2 cans (298 g each) |
| Ketchup | ½ cup | 120 ml |
| Ground nutmeg (optional) | pinch | pinch |

## Instructions

### Mix and Shape
1. Preheat the oven to 325°F / 165°C.
2. In a large bowl, combine the ground chuck, bread crumbs, eggs, and evaporated milk. Mix with your hands until just combined — overworking makes the meatballs dense.
3. Roll into small balls, about 1½ inches / 4 cm across. You should get roughly 30-36.

### Brown
1. Pour enough oil into a large frying pan to coat the bottom and heat over medium-high heat.
2. Working in batches so the pan isn't crowded, fry the meatballs, turning as needed, until browned on all sides, about 6-8 minutes per batch. They do not need to be cooked through — the oven finishes them.
3. Transfer the browned meatballs to a shallow casserole dish, scraping the pan drippings in with them.

### Sauce and Bake
1. In a bowl, stir together the cream of celery soup and ketchup until smooth.
2. Pour the sauce evenly over the meatballs.
3. Sprinkle lightly with nutmeg, if using.
4. Cover with a lid or foil and bake for 30 minutes, until bubbling and the meatballs are cooked through (160°F / 71°C internal).

## Notes

- **Cream of mushroom version.** See [Baked Meatballs in Cream of Mushroom Gravy](baked-meatballs-cream-of-mushroom.md) for the variation made with cream of mushroom soup.
- **Not Swedish meatballs.** The original card called these Swedish meatballs, but there's no allspice, no sour cream, and no roux — it's a mid-century American cream-soup casserole.
- **Don't dilute the soup.** The undiluted soup plus drippings makes the gravy. Adding water or milk thins it out.
- **Only have one can?** One can (10.5 oz / 298 g) plus the ketchup is about 1¾ cups of sauce for 2 lb of meat — enough to glaze the meatballs, but not enough to spoon over noodles or rice. Two ways around it:
  1. **Halve the batch.** Use 1 lb / 450 g ground chuck, 1 egg, ½ cup / 55 g bread crumbs, 2 tbsp / 30 ml evaporated milk, and ¼ cup / 60 ml ketchup with the single can. Yields 4 servings.
  2. **Stretch the can.** Keep the full 2 lb and mix 1 can with ½ cup / 120 ml ketchup plus ½ cup / 120 ml beef broth (milk, cream, or water with a splash of Worcestershire also work). Pack the meatballs into a smaller, deeper dish — 9×9 in / 23×23 cm rather than 9×13 — so the sauce pools instead of spreading thin, and keep it covered for the full bake. Taste the sauce before pouring; stretched sauce may need extra salt.
- **Serve with:** cheese-rice casserole, rice and corn casserole, macaroni and cheese, tossed salad, or buttered vegetables. Also good over egg noodles or mashed potatoes.
- **Make ahead.** Brown the meatballs a day early and refrigerate. Add the sauce and bake straight from the fridge, adding about 10 minutes to the bake time.
