# Chicken & Stuffing Bake with Vegetables

> A fast pantry casserole — canned chicken, cream of chicken soup, and mixed vegetables under a fluffy layer of boxed stuffing.

## Details

| | |
|-----|-----|
| **Source** | Home kitchen |
| **Cuisine** | American |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep Time** | 15 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 25-30 minutes |
| **Bakeware** | 8x8 in (20 cm) baking dish |
| **Storage** | Fridge 3-4 days • Freeze 2 months • Reheat covered at 350°F / 180°C until hot through |

## Ingredients

### Filling
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Canned chicken breast, drained and broken up | 1 can (12.5 oz) | 1 can (354 g) |
| Cream of chicken soup | 1 can (10.5 oz) | 1 can (298 g) |
| Yellow onion, small, diced | 1 | 1 |
| Butter, for the onion | 1 tbsp | 14 g |
| Frozen mixed vegetables (no need to thaw) | 1 to 1½ cups | 140-210 g |
| Milk | ¼ cup | 60 ml |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp | 1 g |

### Stuffing Topping
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Stove Top stuffing mix | 1 box (6 oz) | 1 box (170 g) |
| Water | 1¼ cups | 300 ml |
| Butter | 3 tbsp | 42 g |

## Instructions

### Prep
1. Heat the oven to 350°F / 180°C and grease the baking dish.
2. Sauté the diced onion in the 1 tbsp butter over medium heat for about 5 minutes, until soft and translucent.

### Mix the Filling
1. Stir together the onion, chicken, soup, frozen vegetables, milk, and pepper.
2. Spread the mixture evenly in the prepared dish.

### Make the Stuffing
1. Bring the water and 3 tbsp butter to a boil.
2. Stir in the stuffing mix, cover, and remove from the heat. Let sit 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

### Bake
1. Spoon the stuffing loosely over the filling without packing it down.
2. Bake 25-30 minutes, until bubbling at the edges and golden on top.
3. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- **Don't pack the stuffing.** A loose, mounded layer stays light and crisps on top; pressed down it turns dense and gummy.
- **Frozen vegetables go in frozen.** Thawing first releases water into the filling.
- **Nutrition, per serving (¼ of the pan):** about 460 calories, 25 g protein, 3 g fiber. Assumes about 1 tbsp butter for the onion — using oil or less butter lowers it slightly.
- **Swaps.** Cream of mushroom or cream of celery soup work in place of cream of chicken. Leftover shredded rotisserie chicken (about 2 cups / 280 g) replaces the canned chicken.
