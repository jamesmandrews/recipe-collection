# Crispy Honey Garlic Chicken Thighs

> Skin-on chicken thighs seared until crisp, oven-finished, and brushed with a buttery honey-garlic glaze.

## Details

| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep Time** | 10 minutes |

## Ingredients

### Chicken
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs | 2¼ lb | 1 kg |
| Salt | to taste | to taste |
| Black pepper | to taste | to taste |
| Paprika | 1 tsp | 2 g |
| Garlic powder | ½ tsp | 1.5 g |
| Cornstarch or all-purpose flour | 2 tbsp | 16 g |
| Cooking oil | 2 tbsp | 30 ml |

### Honey Garlic Glaze
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves | 4 cloves |
| Honey | 3 tbsp | 63 g |
| Soy sauce | 2 tbsp | 30 ml |
| Butter | 1 tbsp | 14 g |
| Lemon juice | 1 tbsp | 15 ml |
| Chili flakes (optional) | ½ tsp | 1 g |
| Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish | to taste | to taste |

## Instructions

### Season
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Pat the chicken thighs thoroughly dry with paper towels. Season all over with salt, pepper, paprika, and garlic powder.
3. Dust lightly with cornstarch or flour — a thin coating only, just enough to help the skin crisp.

### Sear and Roast
1. Heat the oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Lay the thighs in skin-side down and cook undisturbed for 6-8 minutes, until the skin is golden and crisp.
2. Flip and cook 3-4 minutes more.
3. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast 15-20 minutes, until the thickest part of the thigh reaches 165°F (74°C).

### Make the Glaze
1. While the chicken roasts, melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and cook about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
2. Stir in the honey, soy sauce, lemon juice, and chili flakes if using. Simmer 2-3 minutes, until glossy and slightly thickened.

### Finish
1. Brush the glaze over the cooked thighs. Go generously on the meat but keep the skin lightly coated so it stays crisp.
2. Garnish with chopped parsley and serve immediately with rice, roasted potatoes, or steamed vegetables.

## Notes

- **Dry skin is the whole game.** Any surface moisture steams instead of browns. Pat the thighs dry right before seasoning, and dry them again if they've been sitting.
- **Go light on the starch.** A heavy dredge turns gummy under the glaze. You want a dusting, not a breading.
- **Glaze at the end, not before.** Honey scorches at searing and roasting temperatures. Brushing after cooking keeps it sweet rather than bitter.
- **The lemon juice isn't optional in spirit.** It cuts the honey and soy so the glaze reads balanced instead of cloying.
